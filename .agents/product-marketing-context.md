# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-05-05*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** FreelanceKit is the all-in-one operating system for freelancers — projects, invoices, taxes, pipeline, and rates, all in one connected kit.

**What it does:** FreelanceKit replaces the 5+ disconnected tools freelancers cobble together to run their business. It combines a project tracker (tasks, Gantt, milestones, time logs, billing, collaboration), invoice builder with aging reports, financial overview, pipeline tracker with weighted revenue forecasting, timesheet with activity heatmap, tax tracker, rate calculator, client directory, and document tools — all under one roof, fully connected.

**Product category:** Freelance business management / all-in-one freelance toolkit

**Product type:** SaaS web app (single-page application at app.freelancekit.io)

**Business model:** Freemium — Free plan (core tools) + Pro plan (full suite). Currently in beta: all users get Pro features for $0, no credit card required.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Freelancers run their business across too many disconnected tools — spreadsheets, separate invoicing apps, basic task apps, notes for meeting minutes — and end up losing time, dropping balls, undercharging, and worrying about cash flow.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Spreadsheets: manual, not connected to projects or invoices, break under complexity
- Generic project tools (Notion, Trello, Asana): no invoicing, no financial tracking, no rates
- Invoicing-only tools (FreshBooks, Wave): no project management, no pipeline, no timesheet
- Paying $40+/month each for separate tools that don't talk to each other

**What it costs them:**
- Time lost switching between apps and re-entering data
- Money lost from undercharging (don't know their real floor rate)
- Late invoices and missed follow-ups (no aging reports)
- Cash flow surprises from unforecast dry months

**Emotional tension:** Stress of not knowing where a project stands; anxiety about whether they're charging enough; fear of a slow month sneaking up; overwhelm from running a business solo

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** No direct single-competitor at the "all-in-one freelance OS" level — falls short because most products solve one problem (invoicing OR project management OR time tracking)

**Secondary:**
- FreshBooks / Wave / HoneyBook — invoicing & accounting, falls short because no project tracker, no pipeline, no rate calculator
- Notion / Trello / Asana — project management, falls short because no invoicing, no financial overview, no tax or rate tools
- Toggl / Harvest — time tracking, falls short because no invoicing, no pipeline, no business overview
- Bonsai / AND.CO — freelance contracts + invoicing, falls short because limited project tracking, no forecasting

**Indirect:**
- Excel/Google Sheets — free, fully manual, no automation, doesn't connect anything
- Doing nothing / winging it — zero cost but maximum stress and missed money

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Every tool a freelancer needs in one connected kit (not just one piece of the puzzle)
- All tools talk to each other — time logs attach to projects, billing phases link to invoices, pipeline forecasts update from the same data
- Built specifically for freelancers/solopreneurs — not adapted from agency or enterprise tools
- Rate Calculator works backwards from take-home target to minimum hourly rate (accounting for taxes, expenses, slow months)
- Pipeline tracker with weighted revenue forecasting — see the dry month before it hits
- Activity heatmap + utilisation tracking for time management
- Free during beta — no credit card, no risk

**How we do it differently:** One flat price (vs. paying $40/mo per tool). Everything connected (vs. copy-pasting between apps). Built for solo freelancers (vs. teams or agencies).

**Why that's better:** Less time managing tools = more time doing billable work. No more blind spots in cash flow. Know your numbers without a spreadsheet.

**Why customers choose us:** "Stop context-switching and start charging what you're worth." Simplicity — one login, one dashboard, everything in one place.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with juggling 5+ separate tools; spreadsheets breaking; forgetting to invoice; undercharging clients; stressed about taxes and cash flow

**Pull:** One place for everything; pipeline forecasting catches dry months early; knowing your floor rate; no more copy-pasting between apps

**Habit:** Already set up in Notion/Trello; have an existing invoicing flow; used to "free" spreadsheets

**Anxiety:** Learning curve of a new tool; fear of data not being accurate in a new system; beta = incomplete?
