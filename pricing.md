# FreelanceKit Pricing

Last updated: 2026-05-06

FreelanceKit offers two plans. Both are currently free during beta — no credit card required for either.

---

## Free Plan

**Price:** $0/month — free forever  
**Credit card required:** No  
**Trial period:** None (permanently free)

**Included:**
- All core business tools
- Client directory (with vCard and CSV import/export)
- Invoice history (view only)
- Project tracker (basic)
- No usage limits on core features

---

## Pro Plan

**Price:** $0/month during beta · $9/month after beta (billed monthly)  
**Credit card required:** No (during beta)  
**Trial period:** Not applicable — full Pro access for $0 during beta

**Included (everything in Free, plus):**
- Invoice Builder — create, send, and export professional invoices as PDF
- Invoice aging reports — see outstanding, overdue, and paid at a glance
- Multi-currency invoice support
- Rate Calculator — calculates minimum hourly floor rate from take-home target, accounting for taxes, expenses, and utilisation
- Tax Tracker — quarterly tax liability estimates from income data
- Pipeline Tracker — Kanban board for prospective clients with weighted revenue forecasting
- Timesheet — time logging with 16-week activity heatmap and weekly utilisation goals
- Financial Overview — 4-tab dashboard (earned income, invoice aging, by-client breakdown, month-over-month trends)
- Project collaboration — invite clients and collaborators to specific projects
- Meeting notes, welcome packet generator, project brief builder

---

## Notes

- No setup fees, no contracts, cancel anytime
- Post-beta pricing: Free plan retains core tools; Pro at $9/month
- Beta pricing applies to all users until the beta period ends; advance notice will be given before any price change
- Both plans: data isolated per account, row-level security, no data selling
